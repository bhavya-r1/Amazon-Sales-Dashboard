# Global Superstore Sales Performance & Business Insights Dashboard

## Project Overview

Developed an interactive Power BI dashboard to analyze sales performance, profitability, product categories, shipping costs, and geographical sales distribution. The dashboard transforms sales data into actionable business insights through interactive visualizations and KPI tracking.

## Tools & Technologies

- Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- Data Cleaning and Transformation
- Data Visualization
- Business Intelligence

## Analysis Performed

- **Category Analysis:** Compared sales performance across Technology, Furniture, and Office Supplies.
- **Geographical Analysis:** Evaluated sales across countries and cities to identify high-performing locations.
- **Shipping Analysis:** Examined sales distribution across Standard Class, Second Class, First Class, and Same Day shipping.
- **Market Analysis:** Compared sales contributions across APAC, EU, US, LATAM, EMEA, Africa, and Canada.
- **Business Performance:** Analyzed sales, profit, quantity, and shipping costs through KPI cards.

## Key Business Insights

- Technology was the leading product category, contributing 4.74M (37.52%) in sales.
- APAC was the highest-performing market, generating 3.59M (28.37%) in sales.
- The United States recorded the highest country-level sales at approximately 2.3M.
- New York City was the leading city, contributing approximately 0.25M in sales.
- Standard Class accounted for the largest share of sales among shipping modes.

## Business Recommendations

- Prioritize inventory and marketing investments in the Technology category.
- Strengthen sales strategies in high-performing markets, particularly APAC.
- Explore growth opportunities in cities with strong sales performance.
- Review shipping costs and delivery strategies to identify opportunities for cost optimization.

## Project Outcome

Created an interactive sales analytics solution that provides visibility into business performance, identifies revenue-driving categories and markets, and supports data-driven decisions around inventory allocation, geographical expansion, and shipping cost optimization.
